About Wanda

Wanda Hope Wyatt was a beloved mother, grandmother, and friend to many. Known for her kindness, strength, and warm smile, she touched the lives of everyone she met. Wanda enjoyed gardening, cooking for her family, and spending quiet afternoons on the porch. Her legacy of love and compassion continues to live on through her children and grandchildren.
