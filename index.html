Why this work matters

A name should still be easy to read.

Weather, moss, and time work on a headstone the same way they work on everything left outdoors — slowly, and without asking permission. A name that used to be spoken every day can fade under decades of lichen and mineral staining until it's hard to make out at all.

Cemetery Revival NC cleans headstones and monuments using methods suited to the specific stone — granite, marble, or bronze — so the surface is restored without being damaged. We work with individual families on a single stone, and with churches and cemetery associations on larger grounds.

How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

1

Free on-site assessment

We look at the stone in person to identify its material and condition before recommending a method.

2

Stone-safe cleaning

We apply the right product for that stone — gently, by hand, with no damage to the surface or engraving.

3

Time to work

Some treatments continue lifting stains gradually over the following weeks — no scrubbing required.

4

Optional tribute page

If you'd like, we'll add a small NFC/QR marker linking to a page with photos and memories.
