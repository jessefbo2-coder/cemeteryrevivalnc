Why this work matters

A name should still be easy to read.

Weather, moss, and time work on a headstone the same way they work on everything left outdoors — slowly, and without asking permission. A name that used to be spoken every day can fade under decades of lichen and mineral staining until it's hard to make out at all.

Cemetery Revival NC cleans headstones and monuments using methods suited to the specific stone — granite, marble, or bronze — so the surface is restored without being damaged. We work with individual families on a single stone, and with churches and cemetery associations on larger grounds.

Services & Pricing

Careful cleaning, straightforward pricing.

Every stone is different. Prices are starting points and may vary based on the stone's size, material, and how dirty or weathered it is. We'll confirm your exact price before any work begins.

For a single family monument or a small group of markers. We take the time to identify the stone and clean it carefully, without pressure washing or harsh abrasives.

Family Plot Cleaning

For multiple stones in the same family plot. Group pricing can make cleaning several markers more affordable, with the final price based on the number, size, material, and condition of the stones.
